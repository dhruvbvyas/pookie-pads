FAQ

Still wondering about something? Call our customer care on +91 98794 30119.

How do I choose between XL and XXL?

Pick XL for regular to medium flow. Pick XXL for medium to heavy flow and overnight, when you'd like extra length. Not sure? Try the fit finder above.

How often should I change my pad?

Most health guidance says every 4 to 6 hours, or sooner if it feels full. Change more often on heavy days. If you have pain, very heavy bleeding or anything that worries you, please talk to a doctor.

How do I pay?

Pay by UPI with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app when you place your order. You'll see your total before you pay.

How does the buy-back promise work?

If the size doesn't suit you or the pads don't feel comfortable, message or call us on +91 98794 30119 with your order number, at any time. There's no time limit. Send back the unused pads, still sealed in their individual wrappers, and we'll refund you for every unused pad by UPI.

How will I know my order is placed?

You'll see your order number on screen right away, and we'll send a confirmation SMS to the mobile number you enter at checkout.

How do I dispose of a used pad?

Fold it up, wrap it in its wrapper or some paper, and put it in the bin. Please don't flush pads: they block pipes.
