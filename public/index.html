How do I choose between XL and XXL?

Pick XL for regular to medium flow. Pick XXL for medium to heavy flow and overnight, when you'd like extra length. Not sure? Try the fit finder above.

How often should I change my pad?

Most health guidance says every 4 to 6 hours, or sooner if it feels full. Change more often on heavy days. If you have pain, very heavy bleeding or anything that worries you, please talk to a doctor.

How do I dispose of a used pad?

Fold it up, wrap it in its wrapper or some paper, and put it in the bin. Please don't flush pads: they block pipes.
